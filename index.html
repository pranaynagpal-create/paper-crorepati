    The rules behind the numbers

    When you exercise

    • The gain on the day you exercise (value per share minus exercise price) is taxed like salary, at your slab plus 4% cess, plus surcharge when the year's income crosses ₹50 L (10%), ₹1 Cr (15%) or ₹2 Cr (25%). The default picks the surcharge band your ESOP gain alone would put you in.
    • Unlisted shares use the FMV from a merchant-banker report not older than 180 days. Listed shares use the market price.
    • Exercising and selling on the same day makes the whole gain salary income. Exercising earlier makes only the gain up to that day salary income, and the rest capital gain. That can net more, but it costs real cash today and the exit may never come.

    Startup deferral

    • An employee of a DPIIT-recognised startup that also holds an Inter-Ministerial Board certificate under Section 140 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (Section 80-IAC of the 1961 Act) pays the same tax later: at the earliest of selling the shares, leaving the company, or 60 months from the end of the tax year of allotment.
    • About 3,700 of roughly 1.97 lakh DPIIT startups held the certificate as of April 2025, so the default is "no". Leaving ends the deferral, so the 18-month number never uses it.

    When you sell

    • Your cost is the value already taxed at exercise, so the same rupee is not taxed twice.
    • Unlisted shares held more than 24 months: 12.5% plus cess, no indexation, no ₹1.25 L exemption. Held 24 months or less: your slab rate.
    • Listed shares held more than 12 months: 12.5% plus cess on gains above ₹1.25 L. Held 12 months or less: 20% plus cess.

    Exit price and vesting

    • Value per share at exit = today's value × growth × (1 minus dilution).
    • Vesting follows your schedule. On a 4-year plan with a 12-month cliff and yearly vesting, 25% has vested at month 18. Monthly vesting after the cliff would give 37.5%.
    • After you leave, most plans give 30 to 90 days to exercise what has vested, paid in cash. Unexercised options lapse. Unvested options are gone the day you leave.
