# LinkedIn post drafts for Paper Crorepati

## Launch post (v1.0)

HR told you your ESOPs are worth ₹1.2 crore.

I built a 30-second calculator that tells you what actually reaches your bank account. Three numbers:

1. On paper: the number from the town hall.
2. In hand: after dilution, exercise price, perquisite tax at your slab, and capital gains tax.
3. Quit at month 18: what vests, the cash you must find within 90 days to keep it, and what you leave behind.

The example grant goes from ₹3.6 crore on paper to ₹1.7 crore in hand. Quit at month 18 and it is ₹55 lakh, but only if you can find ₹11 lakh in cash first. Otherwise, zero.

Before it shows the answer, it asks you for an honest guess. Then it tells you how far off you were. The page keeps an anonymous scoreboard: how many people ran it, and how many guessed too high.

Nothing you type leaves your browser. No login, no email, no names.

Run yours: https://pranaynagpal-create.github.io/paper-crorepati/

Paste your result in the comments, and tell me what the model gets wrong. Buybacks, secondaries, liquidation preference and acceleration are on the patch list. Every good argument becomes v1.1.

Not tax advice. The defaults are an invented grant.
